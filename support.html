Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions & answers.

Everything you need to know about PetNexa AI functionality and privacy.

Is PetNexa AI free to download and use? ▼

Yes. PetNexa AI is free to download and use in Solo Mode. All core pet health management tools—including pet profiles, vaccination logs, deworming tracking, daily care reminders, weight graphs, and local JSON backups—are fully functional without paying a subscription or providing payment credentials.

Can I use PetNexa AI without creating an account? ▼

Yes! By default, PetNexa AI operates in Solo Mode. Solo Mode stores all records exclusively in a local SQLite database on your physical mobile device. No account registration, email address, password, or cloud server connection is required.

What is the difference between Solo Mode and Household Sync? ▼

Solo Mode: 100% offline-first. Your pet records stay exclusively on your device.
Household Sync Mode: An optional cloud feature powered by Supabase. It allows family members or co-caretakers to invite each other to a shared Home and synchronize records across multiple devices.

Does PetNexa AI replace my licensed veterinarian? ▼

No. Absolutely not. PetNexa AI is an educational triage companion and organizational care tool. It does not diagnose illnesses, prescribe medication, or formulate treatment plans. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for medical concerns. In an emergency, transport your pet immediately to an animal emergency clinic.

How do AI credits work and how do I earn more? ▼

Each pet parent receives 3 starter AI credits per week for symptom evaluations. If you exhaust your credits, you can optionally watch a short rewarded video ad to earn an additional credit (up to 3 rewarded ads per week). Your credit allotment automatically refreshes every week.

Is my pet's medical record used to train AI models? ▼

No. Symptom consultations and optical document scans are processed ephemerally using Google Gemini Flash and Groq inference. Payloads exist in RAM only for the duration of the request and are never stored or used to train or refine public AI models.

How do I back up or transfer my records to a new phone? ▼

1. On your current phone: open Settings → Backup & Restore (JSON) → Export Data to save a portable .json file.
2. Transfer that file to your new device (via Quick Share, email, Google Drive, or USB).
3. On your new phone: open PetNexa AI, go to Settings → Backup & Restore (JSON) → Restore Data (Replace Mode), and select your backup file.

Common Troubleshooting Guides

Step-by-step guidance to resolve common technical issues on Android devices:

1. Not Receiving Reminder Notifications

• Open your device Settings → Apps → PetNexa AI → Notifications and ensure notifications are enabled.
• Check that Daily Summary Time is configured in app Settings (default: 08:00 AM).
• On Android, ensure PetNexa AI is exempted from aggressive battery optimization / sleep modes.

2. Household Sync Showing "Needs Sync"

• Ensure your mobile device has an active Wi-Fi or cellular internet connection.
• Open Settings → Data & Cloud Sync and tap Sync Household Data.
• If the issue persists, verify that the Home invitation code was accepted by your family member.

3. AI Assistant Connection Error

• Verify that your device has an active internet connection (AI triage is an online-only service).
• Check that you have at least 1 AI credit remaining on the AI Assistant screen.
• If an inference endpoint experiences temporary latency, wait 30 seconds and try again.

4. OCR Document Text Not Detected

• Ensure the veterinary invoice or vaccination certificate is placed on a flat, well-lit surface.
• Avoid glare, heavy shadows, or blurry camera angles.
• Ensure the text is written in English or Latin script and includes standard dates (YYYY-MM-DD).
