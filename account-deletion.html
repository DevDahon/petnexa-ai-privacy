User Rights & Google Play Compliance

PetNexa AI Account & Data Deletion Center

We believe you should have complete control over your personal and pet health information. In compliance with Google Play Developer policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and CCPA, this page explains the exact procedures for deleting your account, cloud records, and local device data.

Method 1: In-App Self-Service Deletion

Instant Purge

The fastest and most direct method. If you still have the PetNexa AI mobile application installed on your device, you can trigger an immediate deletion without waiting for manual processing:

1
Open the PetNexa AI mobile application on your device.
2
Tap the More tab in the bottom navigation bar and select Settings.
3
Scroll down to the Data & Cloud Sync section.
4
For Cloud Accounts (Household Sync): Tap Delete PetNexa Account.
For Offline Solo Users: Tap Clear Local Device Data to wipe your local SQLite database.
5
Confirm the deletion prompt dialog.

Immediate Processing

In-app deletion invokes an immediate server-side Supabase RPC function that deletes your authentication identity, revokes all session tokens, and permanently deletes all pet records belonging to your household.

Method 2: External Email Deletion Request

Within 30 Days

If you have uninstalled the application, lost access to your mobile device, or prefer to submit a manual deletion request, you can email our support team directly:

1
Send an email to: leaves018@gmail.com
2
Use the subject line: [PetNexa Account Deletion Request]
3
Include your Registered Account Email (the email address used for Household Sync OTP or Google sign-in) and your Owner Display Name.
4
Include the explicit confirmation statement: "I request the permanent deletion of my PetNexa AI account and all associated cloud data."

Ownership Verification & SLA

To protect against unauthorized deletion, we will send a confirmation reply to the registered email address. Once confirmed, your account and cloud data will be permanently wiped within 30 calendar days (typically completed within 72 business hours).

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Transparency is critical. When you initiate an account or data deletion, here is how each category of information is treated:

Data Category Storage Location Deletion Action Timeline
User Account & Auth Tokens Supabase Authentication Permanently deleted; auth UID purged Immediate (in-app) / 30 days (email)
Household Memberships & Invites Supabase PostgreSQL Permanently revoked and purged Immediate (in-app) / 30 days (email)
Cloud Pet Profiles & Notes Supabase PostgreSQL Permanently wiped via Row Level Security cascade Immediate (in-app) / 30 days (email)
Medical Records & Reminders Supabase PostgreSQL Permanently deleted Immediate (in-app) / 30 days (email)
Uploaded Photos & Document Crops Supabase Private Storage Buckets Permanently purged from storage buckets Immediate (in-app) / 30 days (email)
Local Device Database Device Physical SQLite Sandboxed File Wiped immediately when tapping "Clear Local Device Data" or uninstalling app Immediate (on-device)
Google AdMob Telemetry Google Advertising Servers Non-identifying ad logs expire per Google's standard retention (up to 30 days) Automatic expiration

Important: Export Your Records Before Deletion

Account and data deletion is irreversible. Once executed, your synchronized pet profiles, vaccination records, and weight logs cannot be restored.

If you wish to retain an offline record of your pet's medical history for your personal records or veterinary visits, we strongly recommend exporting a portable JSON backup prior to deletion:

How to Export Your Pet Health Records

1. Open PetNexa AI → tap More → Settings.
2. Scroll down to Data & Cloud Sync.
3. Tap Backup & Restore (JSON).
4. Tap Export Data to save or share your complete JSON v2 health file.
