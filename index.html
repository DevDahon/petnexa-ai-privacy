1. Introduction & Application Architecture

PetNexa AI is an intelligent pet health management and care companion mobile application developed and maintained by Dahon. The application is distributed for Android (Google Play Store), iOS, and Web.

The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to clearly, truthfully, and comprehensively disclose how PetNexa AI handles user information, what information is stored locally on your device versus synchronized to cloud services, how artificial intelligence features operate, and how you can exercise full control over your personal information and pet health data.

PetNexa AI is engineered around a two-tier operational architecture designed to give users complete autonomy over their data posture:

Solo Mode (Default Local-First)

When using PetNexa AI in Solo Mode, all owner profile details, pet profiles, vaccination records, weight tracking logs, medical notes, reminders, and consultation history reside exclusively on your physical device in a native SQLite database (or secure browser storage on web). No account registration, login credentials, or cloud server connections are required. Your data remains completely under your personal physical custody.

Household Sync Mode (Optional Cloud)

Users who wish to collaborate with family members or co-caretakers can optionally enable Household Sync. This mode connects to a cloud backend hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security) via email OTP or Google OAuth authentication. Selected pets, health records, reminders, and media attachments are securely synchronized among authorized members of that specific Home.

Online-Only Connected Features

Certain advanced features strictly require an active internet connection to execute: AI symptom consultations, AI veterinary document scanning (OCR), nearby clinic discovery (Find a Vet), and rewarded video ads. These features transmit minimal, purpose-specific payloads over encrypted HTTPS connections.

2. Information We Collect

PetNexa AI adheres strictly to data minimization principles. We collect and process only the information necessary to provide the application's verified features. We do not collect or monitor data based on conjecture or unverified features.

2.1 Information Directly Provided by You

Owner Profile & Settings

Information: Full display name, date of birth (used strictly to verify that the user meets the minimum age requirement of 13+), daily summary notification time preference (e.g., 08:00 AM), preferred theme mode (Light, Dark, or System), and interface language.

Pet Profiles

Information: Pet name, species (Dog, Cat, Bird, Rabbit, etc.), breed, biological sex, birth date, weight tracking log (date, weight in kilograms), coat color, optional microchip identification number, assigned veterinary clinic, care notes, and profile avatar photos.

Medical Records & Care Reminders

Information: Health visit category (Vaccination, Deworming, Medication, Surgery, Checkup, Grooming, Allergy, Lab Test, Other), visit date, clinic name, attending veterinarian, medical notes, prescribed dosage, medication batch/lot numbers, weight at visit, next follow-up date, and photo attachments. Care reminders include task title, recurrence, due date, linked pet ID, and completion status.

AI Consultation Inputs

Information: Pet context (species, breed, age, weight) combined with user-reported symptom descriptions, onset duration, frequency, appetite level, water intake, behavioral changes, mobility, breathing status, injury presence, additional observations, and preferred response language.

Uploaded Documents & Media

Information: Photos captured via your device camera or selected from your photo library representing pet avatars, medical record attachments, and veterinary clinic invoices or prescription documents submitted for optical text scanning.

AI Response Safety Reports

Information: If you choose to report an AI consultation guidance response, the consultation ID, guidance excerpt, risk level, user-selected report reason (unsafe, offensive, incorrect, or other), optional text comment, and a hashed installation identifier are submitted for moderation.

2.2 System-Generated and Device Information

  • Foreground Location Coordinates: When you open the Find a Vet map, approximate or precise latitude and longitude coordinates are requested solely in the foreground to center the map and query nearby clinics from the OpenStreetMap Overpass API. Your location is never accessed in the background.
  • Community Clinic Directory Submissions: When a user voluntarily submits or confirms a local veterinary clinic, the clinic's public business name, address, telephone number, geographic coordinates, and an anonymous contributor UUID (stored in device AsyncStorage) are submitted to Supabase to prevent duplicate spam submissions.
  • Diagnostic Error Logs (Local Only): If enabled in Settings, the app maintains a local FIFO ring buffer of up to 80 error events (timestamp, error category, sanitized error message) strictly in device storage. Diagnostic logs are never transmitted automatically to any server; they are exported only if you manually choose to export and attach them in an email to support.
  • Pseudonymous Identifiers & Rate Limiting: A cryptographic installation hash derived from limited network request headers is used by backend Edge Functions to enforce rate limiting and prevent denial-of-service abuse. Session authentication tokens are stored securely using Expo SecureStore on native devices.
Explicit Exclusion

What PetNexa AI Does NOT Collect

To avoid any ambiguity regarding device capabilities, PetNexa AI explicitly does NOT collect, process, or monitor:

  • Audio Recordings or Microphone Data: Microphone access (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO) is explicitly blocked in our application configuration. PetNexa AI has zero ability to record audio.
  • Background Location Tracking: The application does not request background location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION). Location queries terminate when the map view is closed.
  • Third-Party Crash Analytics SDKs: PetNexa AI contains zero automated telemetry or user-tracking SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no Sentry, no Mixpanel, no Bugsnag).
  • Payment Cards or Financial Data: PetNexa AI does not process subscriptions or in-app purchases. Rewarded ads provide credits without financial transactions.
  • Human Health Data: Medical records managed in PetNexa AI relate strictly to domestic pets and companion animals.
  • Biometric Identifiers: The application does not collect fingerprints, facial recognition scans, or biometric data.

3. How We Use Information

We process collected information only for legitimate, transparent purposes directly tied to the functionality of PetNexa AI:

  • Local Pet Care Management: Store, calculate pet ages, organize medical timelines, track vaccination validity, monitor weight trends, and maintain structured health profiles on your device.
  • Preventive Care Reminders: Trigger on-device notifications for scheduled feedings, medications, vaccinations, and veterinary appointments based on your local device clock.
  • Real-Time AI Symptom Evaluation: Submit pet context and user-described symptoms to cloud AI inference engines to generate structured, non-diagnostic wellness insights and immediate emergency escalation warnings.
  • Optical Document Digitization (OCR): Transmit captured clinic invoices and prescription photos to cloud vision models to automatically extract clinic names, dates, vaccinations, dosages, and follow-up schedules.
  • Nearby Clinic Discovery: Query OpenStreetMap Overpass API using foreground map coordinates to locate nearby veterinary hospitals, animal clinics, and emergency facilities.
  • Household Multi-Member Sync: For users who opt into Household Sync, replicate pet profiles, medical history, reminders, and media attachments across approved furparent accounts linked by invite code.
  • Ad-Supported AI Credit Meter: Display optional, non-personalized rewarded video advertisements via Google Mobile Ads to allow users to refill weekly AI consultation credits without payment.
  • Security, Abuse Prevention & Safety Moderation: Enforce API request rate limits, verify user age requirements (13+), prevent community clinic directory manipulation, and review user-flagged AI responses for accuracy and safety.
  • User-Initiated Technical Support: Review user-provided descriptions and manually attached diagnostic logs when resolving inquiries submitted to our support email.

4. AI-Powered Features and Data Processing

PetNexa AI incorporates artificial intelligence to deliver symptom guidance and document OCR extraction. Because transparency in AI data handling is critical, we disclose our exact technical architecture:

Inference Providers & Routing

AI features are powered by Google Gemini (model gemini-3.5-flash via the Google Generative AI API) with an automated fallback to Groq (model openai/gpt-oss-20b via Groq Cloud API). All requests are proxied securely through Supabase Edge Functions (Deno runtime). API keys reside strictly on backend servers; the mobile client never holds raw provider credentials.

Ephemeral Processing & Zero Training

All inputs submitted to AI endpoints (symptom details, pet context, or base64 document images) are processed ephemerally in real time over TLS/HTTPS. Under Google Cloud Generative AI and Groq developer API terms, customer API data is never used to train or fine-tune foundation models. Neither provider retains ownership or training rights over your submitted data.

No Cloud Conversation Storage

The Supabase Edge Functions do not log, persist, or store your consultation prompts, responses, or scanned images in a central cloud database during ordinary inference. Your consultation history is saved strictly on your local device (and synced to your private Household database if Household Sync is active).

Safety Protocol

Deterministic Red-Flag Emergency Triage

Before and alongside AI inference, PetNexa AI executes deterministic, rule-based emergency triage algorithms. If your input contains life-threatening keywords (such as breathing distress, sudden collapse, seizures, severe bleeding, or suspected poisoning), the application immediately triggers an Emergency classification and instructs you to transport your pet to an emergency veterinary clinic immediately without relying on home monitoring.

Safety Reporting Mechanism

Every AI consultation response features a reporting action (flag icon). If you encounter guidance that you consider unsafe, offensive, or incorrect, you can submit a report. The consultation ID, excerpt, risk level, report category, optional user comment, and a hashed installation identifier are stored in the ai_response_reports table for developer safety audits.

Crucial Medical Limitations

PetNexa AI is an informational tool, not a licensed veterinarian. AI guidance does not constitute veterinary medical diagnosis, prognosis, treatment plans, or prescription medication dosages. The AI is specifically instructed never to prescribe medications, provide dosage instructions, or recommend fasting periods. Always consult a qualified, licensed veterinarian for animal health concerns.

6. Data Storage and Security Practices

PetNexa AI implements comprehensive, industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security measures to protect user data:

Local Device Security

In Solo mode, data is isolated inside your operating system's private app sandbox in native SQLite. Sensitive authentication tokens and cryptographic installation keys utilize Expo SecureStore (backed by Android Keystore and iOS Keychain). Android builds enforce allowBackup: false to prevent unauthorized extraction via physical ADB backup bridges.

Cloud Tenant Isolation (RLS)

In Household Sync mode, Supabase PostgreSQL tables strictly enforce Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Database rows are accessible exclusively to authenticated users who possess verified membership in that specific Home. Cross-tenant access is structurally prohibited at the database engine level.

Private Signed Storage Assets

Media files uploaded in Household mode (pet photos and medical attachments) are stored in private Supabase Storage buckets (petnexa-home-assets). Files are never publicly accessible via static URLs; they are retrieved strictly through authenticated, short-lived signed URLs generated on demand.

Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application, Supabase infrastructure, Google AI endpoints, and OpenStreetMap servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).

Binary Hardening: Production Android application binaries are compiled with full R8 code shrinking, dead-code elimination, and DEX optimization to prevent reverse-engineering and tampering.

Security Disclaimer: While we implement industry-standard defenses and continuously audit our security architecture, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% impenetrable.

7. Data Retention Policy

We retain personal and pet health information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes described in this policy:

  • Local Device Records: Stored continuously on your device until you manually modify or delete individual items, restore an overwriting backup file, wipe local data via Settings, or uninstall the application.
  • Household Cloud Records: Retained in Supabase for the duration of your active Household account. Soft-deleted records are retained temporarily to ensure sync consistency across offline devices and are purged permanently upon account deletion.
  • AI & OCR Payloads: Ephemeral in transit. Prompts, uploaded receipt images, and generated inferences are not retained in a persistent central database during standard consultations.
  • AI Safety Reports: Retained in the ai_response_reports table for up to 90 days following submission to enable safety investigation and model evaluation.
  • Local Diagnostic Event Logs: Maintained in a local FIFO ring buffer strictly capped at 80 events on device. Cleared whenever the user taps "Clear Diagnostics" or "Clear Local Device Data".
  • Community Clinic Submissions: Public clinic directory records (name, address, phone, coordinates) remain in the directory to benefit the community. Pseudonymous contributor UUIDs are retained to prevent vote manipulation.

11. Dedicated Account and Data Deletion Policy

PetNexa AI provides transparent, immediate, and permanent mechanisms to delete your account, personal details, pet health records, and associated cloud assets. Whether you use Solo mode or Household cloud sync, you have the right to permanently purge your data.

1

In-App Self-Service Deletion (Instant)

Signed-in Household users can permanently delete their cloud account directly inside the app:

Settings > Data & Cloud Sync > Tap "Delete PetNexa Account" > Confirm "DELETE".

This triggers an automated call to our secure delete-account backend function, which immediately:

  • Permanently deletes your account and credentials from Supabase Auth.
  • Removes household memberships and deletes owned Home database rows.
  • Purges all uploaded pet photos and medical attachments from private Supabase Storage (petnexa-home-assets).
  • Clears local SQLite database records, secure installation tokens, and cached storage on device.

Solo Mode Local Data Erasure

Go to Settings > Data & Cloud Sync > Tap "Clear Local Device Data" > Confirm to wipe all pets, records, and reminders instantly, or clear application storage in your Android/iOS system settings, or uninstall the app.
