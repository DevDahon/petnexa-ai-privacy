1. Introduction & Application Architecture

PetNexa AI is an intelligent pet health management and care companion mobile application developed and maintained by Dahon. The application is distributed for Android (Google Play Store), iOS, and Web.

The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to clearly, truthfully, and comprehensively disclose how PetNexa AI handles user information, what information is stored locally on your device versus synchronized to cloud services, how artificial intelligence features operate, and how you can exercise full control over your personal information and pet health data.

PetNexa AI is engineered around a two-tier operational architecture designed to give users complete autonomy over their data posture:

Solo Mode (Default Local-First)

When using PetNexa AI in Solo Mode, all owner profile details, pet profiles, vaccination records, weight tracking logs, medical notes, reminders, and consultation history reside exclusively on your physical device in a native SQLite database (or secure browser storage on web). No account registration, login credentials, or cloud server connections are required. Your data remains completely under your personal physical custody.

Household Sync Mode (Optional Cloud)

Users who wish to collaborate with family members or co-caretakers can optionally enable Household Sync. This mode connects to a cloud backend hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row Level Security) via email OTP or Google OAuth authentication. Selected pets, health records, reminders, and media attachments are securely synchronized among authorized members of that specific Home.

Online-Only Connected Features

Certain advanced features strictly require an active internet connection to execute: AI symptom consultations, AI veterinary document scanning (OCR), nearby clinic discovery (Find a Vet), and rewarded video ads. These features transmit minimal, purpose-specific payloads over encrypted HTTPS connections.

2. Information We Collect

PetNexa AI adheres strictly to data minimization principles. We collect and process only the information necessary to provide the application's verified features. We do not collect or monitor data based on conjecture or unverified features.

2.1 Information Directly Provided by You

Owner Profile & Settings

Information: Full display name, date of birth (used strictly to verify that the user meets the minimum age requirement of 13+), daily summary notification time preference (e.g., 08:00 AM), preferred theme mode (Light, Dark, or System), and interface language.

Pet Profiles

Information: Pet name, species (Dog, Cat, Bird, Rabbit, etc.), breed, biological sex, birth date, weight tracking log (date, weight in kilograms), coat color, optional microchip identification number, assigned veterinary clinic, care notes, and profile avatar photos.

Medical Records & Care Reminders

Information: Health visit category (Vaccination, Deworming, Medication, Surgery, Checkup, Grooming, Allergy, Lab Test, Other), visit date, clinic name, attending veterinarian, medical notes, prescribed dosage, medication batch/lot numbers, weight at visit, next follow-up date, and photo attachments. Care reminders include task title, recurrence, due date, linked pet ID, and completion status.

AI Consultation Inputs

Information: Pet context (species, breed, age, weight) combined with user-reported symptom descriptions, onset duration, frequency, appetite level, water intake, behavioral changes, mobility, breathing status, injury presence, additional observations, and preferred response language.

Uploaded Documents & Media

Information: Photos captured via your device camera or selected from your photo library representing pet avatars, medical record attachments, and veterinary clinic invoices or prescription documents submitted for optical text scanning.

AI Response Safety Reports

Information: If you choose to report an AI consultation guidance response, the consultation ID, guidance excerpt, risk level, user-selected report reason (unsafe, offensive, incorrect, or other), optional text comment, and a hashed installation identifier are submitted for moderation.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically or via System Permissions

  • Precise / Coarse Location (Foreground Only): When using the Find a Vet discovery map, your device's geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude) are accessed strictly while the screen is visible to compute a search bounding box for nearby veterinary clinics. Coordinates are never stored on any remote server, never tracked in the background, and never tied to your user profile.
  • Diagnostic & Error Data (Optional / User-Controlled): If you enable the diagnostics toggle in Settings, anonymous client runtime error events and crash details may be logged to help diagnose app instability. Diagnostics can be toggled off at any time.
  • Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob): When watching rewarded video advertisements to earn AI credits, Google AdMob processes standard mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / IDFA), IP address, and interaction metrics subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

What PetNexa AI Does NOT Collect

We do not collect government identification numbers, financial credit card numbers, background GPS tracking logs, audio recordings, biometric data, or contacts lists. We do not access photos outside of the specific images you explicitly choose to upload.

3. How We Use Information

We process collected information solely for legitimate, user-directed functional purposes:

  • Pet Health Management: Storing and presenting vaccination histories, deworming cycles, weight trends, and medical documentation.
  • Local Notification Delivery: Scheduling and triggering local device care alarms and daily summaries for scheduled pet tasks.
  • AI Symptom Evaluation: Analyzing user-submitted symptom observations to generate preliminary triage guidance and emergency warnings.
  • OCR Document Parsing: Extracting veterinary visit dates, clinic names, medications, and next due dates from uploaded clinic invoices.
  • Veterinary Clinic Discovery: Querying public map infrastructure to display nearby animal hospitals and emergency clinics.
  • Household Collaboration: Synchronizing pet data among authorized members of an invited Household via Supabase.
  • Safety & Compliance: Moderating reported AI responses and enforcing our 13+ age requirement.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or pet medical records to data brokers, marketing affiliates, or pharmaceutical advertisers.

4. Artificial Intelligence Processing & Architecture

PetNexa AI provides intelligent symptom triage and document scanning capabilities. Transparency regarding how AI models process your data is central to our mission:

Primary Model: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash

Used for symptom analysis and document extraction. Requests are processed ephemerally over encrypted TLS endpoints. Under Google's API terms, data sent via the Gemini API is not used to train Google foundation models.

Fallback Model: Groq (Llama-3.3-70b-versatile)

If Google AI endpoints experience downtime or latency spikes, PetNexa AI seamlessly fails over to high-speed Groq inference endpoints. Payloads are processed ephemerally with zero retention for model fine-tuning.

Zero Training Data Retention

Neither Google AI nor Groq retains your pet health queries, uploaded invoices, or personal notes to train, retrain, or improve public machine learning models. Processing occurs in memory during the request and response lifecycle and is discarded immediately upon response completion.

Red-Flag Emergency Detection

PetNexa AI features client-side and server-side safety logic that intercepts high-risk keywords (e.g., severe breathing difficulty, seizures, collapse, poison ingestion). When detected, the system immediately advises the user to seek immediate emergency veterinary care and provides direct dialing shortcuts to emergency animal clinics.

AI Response Safety Reporting

If an AI-generated guidance message appears inaccurate, misleading, or unsafe, users can tap the Report button within the consultation details view. Reports are transmitted to our administrative moderation queue for audit and prompt engineering refinement.

6. Data Storage, Architecture & Security

PetNexa AI employs enterprise-grade defensive security measures to protect your pet health records at rest and in transit:

  • Local-First SQLite Storage: By default, all records reside in a sandboxed SQLite database on your physical device. The database is private to the application process and inaccessible to other third-party apps on your phone.
  • Supabase Row Level Security (RLS): For users who opt into Household Cloud Sync, data is stored in PostgreSQL databases protected by fine-grained Row Level Security policies. Users can only read and write records belonging to households where they hold verified membership.
  • Encrypted Hardware Storage: Sensitive authentication tokens and session keys are persisted using device hardware keystores (Expo SecureStore / Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Transit Encryption (TLS 1.3): All network communications with Supabase, Google AI, Groq, and OpenStreetMap are strictly encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2+ / TLS 1.3).
  • Android Backup Restriction (allowBackup: false): Android system-level ADB and Google Drive cloud backups are disabled in our application manifest to prevent extraction of unencrypted local SQLite databases during physical device transfers.
  • Code Obfuscation (R8 DEX Shrinking): Release builds are compiled with Android R8 shrinking and code obfuscation to defend against reverse-engineering, tampering, and intellectual property theft.

7. Data Retention Policy

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide application functionality or comply with legitimate user instructions:

Local SQLite Records

Retained indefinitely on your physical device until you delete individual records, tap Clear Local Device Data in Settings, or uninstall the application from your device.

Household Cloud Sync Records

Retained in active Supabase PostgreSQL storage until you delete a pet or record, exit the household, or initiate an account deletion. Deleted records are immediately purged from active database tables.

AI Consultation & OCR Payloads

Zero retention by AI providers. Consultation inputs and OCR document images are processed ephemerally in RAM and discarded immediately upon response completion.

AdMob Telemetry & Logs

Retained by Google AdMob according to Google's standard ad platform data retention schedules (typically aggregated and anonymized within 30 to 90 days).

8. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal information or pet data to third parties, except in the following strictly limited circumstances:

  • Household Members You Explicitly Invite: When you share a Household Sync invite code with family members or co-caretakers, they receive access to view, edit, and contribute pet records within that shared Household.
  • Verified Service Providers: Encrypted payloads transmitted to the infrastructure vendors listed in Section 5 strictly for service operation.
  • Legal Compliance & Protection: If required to do so by a binding court order, subpoena, or applicable law, or to protect the vital safety of individuals.

10. User Rights & Data Management (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic location, PetNexa AI grants you comprehensive control over your personal and pet health information:

  • Right of Access & Inspection: You can view all saved pet records, reminders, and owner information at any time directly in the app.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit and update owner profiles, pet details, weights, and medical records whenever changes occur.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): You can permanently erase all local device data via Settings → Clear Local Device Data, or delete your entire cloud account and synced household data via Settings → Delete PetNexa Account.
  • Right to Data Portability (Full JSON Export): You can export your entire pet health dossier, medical records, reminders, and vet contacts into a standardized JSON v2 backup file at any time via Settings → Backup & Restore (JSON) → Export Data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing & Withdraw Consent: You can disable cloud sync, toggle off diagnostics, and refuse camera/location permissions without losing access to core offline pet management features.

11. Dedicated Account & Data Deletion

In full compliance with Google Play Developer policies, we provide transparent, immediate, and accessible methods to permanently delete your account and associated data. For complete step-by-step guidance, please review our dedicated Account Deletion Guide.

Method 1: In-App Self-Service (Instant)

1. Open PetNexa AI → tap More → Settings.
2. Scroll down to Data & Cloud Sync.
3. Tap Delete PetNexa Account (for Household Cloud accounts) or Clear Local Device Data (for Solo Mode).
4. Confirm the prompt. Cloud records and auth credentials are wiped immediately.

Method 2: External Email Request

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, send an email to leaves018@gmail.com with the subject [PetNexa Account Deletion Request] specifying your registered account email. All associated cloud records will be purged within 30 days.

12. Children's Privacy

PetNexa AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. During initial owner profile setup, the application enforces a strict age-eligibility check requiring users to be at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under 13, we will promptly delete that information from our records.

17. Terms of Service & Medical Disclaimers

Important Veterinary Medical Disclaimer

PetNexa AI is an informational and organizational care tool. It is NOT a substitute for licensed veterinary medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or medication prescription. Always consult a licensed veterinarian for medical evaluation. Never disregard or delay seeking professional veterinary care because of AI-generated guidance or app notifications.

Emergency Veterinary Protocols
